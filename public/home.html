About Platform

About TecSubtitution

A dedicated institutional solution tailored for modern academic scheduling and faculty duty coordination.

TecSubtitution is a web-based faculty timetable and substitution management system designed to reduce the manual effort involved in managing academic schedules. It provides a centralized platform for timetable management, faculty substitutions, attendance and AI-assisted timetable processing.

📅

Centralized Scheduling

Maintain consistent, structured schedules across departments with instant digital lookup.

🔄

Conflict-Free Adjustments

Smart availability logic accurately identifies free faculty for duty substitutions in seconds.

📑

Automated Digitization

Transform paper sheets, PDFs, and spreadsheets into clean interactive timetables with AI assistance.

Workflow

How TecSubtitution Works

A seamless 5-step workflow ensuring precision, validation, and human review at every stage.

01

Upload Timetable

Upload physical sheets, photos, PDFs, or spreadsheet files containing department schedules.

➔
02

AI-Assisted Extraction

AI-assisted visual parsing identifies periods, day columns, breaks, and teacher-subject slots.

➔
03

Validate Information

Automated validation checks detect missing slots, timing mismatches, and potential conflicts.

➔
04

Review & Edit

Faculty and administrators review the staged preview grid and make any necessary manual edits.

➔
05

Apply to Master Timetable

Approved schedule data is committed to the master database for real-time duty management.

INTELLIGENT PROCESSING

AI-Assisted Timetable Processing

TecSubtitution uses AI-assisted processing to help convert timetable documents into structured digital schedules. Extracted information is validated and presented in an editable preview before it is applied to the master timetable.

🛡️
Human-in-the-Loop Verification: Extracted results always undergo validation and editable review to ensure complete schedule reliability.
🔒
Enterprise Security: All extraction logic runs safely on protected backend routes without client-side API exposure.
